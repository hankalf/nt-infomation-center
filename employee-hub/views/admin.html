Tiles

The big buttons on the kiosk home screen. Paste a monday.com form share link into each one; forms open inside the hub and the kiosk returns home afterwards.

    Board panels

    Live lists read from your monday.com boards, shown on the right of the home screen — for example forklifts that are out of service, or today's checklist submissions. Anyone who can open the hub can see what you choose to show.
